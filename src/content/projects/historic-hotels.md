This collection of maps showing the geographic origin of historic resort and hotel visitors was created as part of a cartography course at Penn State University.

### Design Elements
- **Period Cartography**: Aged parchment textures and vintage typography consistent with late 19th-century styling.
- **Border Treatment**: Hand-drawn aesthetic border shading for Pennsylvania and New York county lines.
- **Flow Visualization**: Visualizing guest travel origins using historical registry logs.
