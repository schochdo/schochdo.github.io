Due to its dry climate, steep topography, and extensive timber stands, the Salmon-Challis National Forest experiences frequent large wildfires.

### Analytical Purpose
- **Fuel Load Modeling**: Predicting fire propagation risk by mapping historical burn intervals.
- **Temporal Decadal Categorization**: Color-coded perimeters representing fire events across four decades.
- **Resource Management**: Supporting Forest Service fire management and rehabilitation strategies.
