An interactive 3D geographic atlas and comprehensive directory cataloging historic opera houses, royal court theaters, and festival stages worldwide.

### Features & Architecture
- **Georeferenced Directory**: Historic opera houses and world premiere theaters cataloged with architectural and historical metadata.
- **Multidimensional Filtering**: Filter venues by theater category, historical era, composer, and architect.
- **Interactive 3D Globe**: Spherical 3D globe interface with smooth rotation, vector boundaries, and venue profiles.
