Isochrone drive-time analysis created to evaluate and optimize school bus routing across the Solanco School District in Lancaster County, PA.

### Analysis Factors
- **Isochrone Rings**: Multi-tier drive-time bands calculated from road network impedance.
- **Route Optimization**: Balancing student ride durations against fleet operation costs.
- **Scheduling Coordination**: Factoring bell schedules, transfer stops, and rural road conditions.
