While working as an Americorps intern in the Salmon-Challis National Forest in Idaho, I created a comprehensive hiking map distributed by the Forest Service at local visitor centers.

### Key Map Highlights
- **Elevation Profiles**: Detailed gradient profiles along primary backcountry trails.
- **Trail Distances**: Segment lengths measured between major intersections and landmarks.
- **Topographic Relief**: Shaded relief highlighting the rugged terrain of the Frank Church–River of No Return Wilderness.
