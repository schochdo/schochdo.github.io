An exploratory computational linguistics and digital humanities tool designed to translate, analyze, and contextualize historical operatic texts and librettos through machine learning.

### Project Overview
Traditional bilingual librettos provide static, literal side-by-side text that often loses idiomatic nuance, poetic meter, dramatic subtext, and historical socio-cultural connotations. This project employs modern natural language processing (NLP) models to provide multi-layered, interactive translation:

- **Idiomatic & Poetic Alignment**: Dynamic cross-referencing between archaic Italian/German idioms and contemporary contextual meaning.
- **Dramaturgical Analysis**: Tokenization, semantic sentiment curves, and character dialog networks visualizable scene by scene.
- **Interactive Reading Environment**: A responsive web interface allowing musicologists, performers, and audiences to toggle between literal glosses, poetic translations, and lexical commentary.
