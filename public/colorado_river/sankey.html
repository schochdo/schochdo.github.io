Upper Basin Sources (12.40 MAF Supply)

Over 90% of all basin runoff originates as high-elevation snowpack in Colorado, Wyoming, and Utah. The Upper Colorado mainstem (6.80 MAF), Green River (3.90 MAF), and San Juan (1.70 MAF) feed Upper Basin agricultural and Front Range trans-mountain diversions (4.50 MAF) before passing Lees Ferry (7.90 MAF).

Annual Storage Drawdown (2.05 MAF)

The red band entering the Lower Basin represents annual storage drawdown. Lower Basin consumptive use, Mexico treaty deliveries, and reservoir evaporation total 9.95 MAF per year, while Lees Ferry releases average 7.90 MAF. The basin bridges this 2.05 MAF deficit by drawing down storage in Lake Powell and Lake Mead.

Consumptive Fate & Evaporation (14.45 MAF)

Water splits between agriculture (~57%: 8.27 MAF) (Western Slope 4.00, Imperial Valley 2.80, Arizona Ag 1.47), municipal cities (3.10 MAF) (CAP 1.35, SoCal CRA 1.00, Front Range 0.50, Las Vegas 0.25), the 1944 Mexico Treaty (1.50 MAF), and evaporation & transit loss (1.58 MAF).
