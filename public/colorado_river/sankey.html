Colorado River: Water Budget & Basin Deficit Sankey Million Acre-Feet / Year (MAF/yr)

Hydrologic Era:
Natural Inflows (Supply)

The Colorado mainstem (~9.0 MAF), Green River (~4.9 MAF), and San Juan River (~2.1 MAF) produce over 90% of basin yield from high-altitude snowpack. In modern megadrought conditions, natural runoff recedes to ~12.4 MAF/yr.

The Structural Deficit (Storage Drawdown)

Consumptive use and system losses (~14.4 MAF/yr) exceed modern natural replenishment (~12.4 MAF/yr). The resulting ~2.0 MAF/yr deficit is physically satisfied by mining storage from Lake Mead and Lake Powell, driving active pools down toward dead pool.

Consumptive Outflows & Losses

Lower Basin diversions (All-American Canal 2.90 MAF, CAP 1.50 MAF, CRA 1.05 MAF), Mexico Treaty delivery (1.50 MAF), Upper Basin uses (4.50 MAF), and reservoir evaporation (~1.58 MAF) account for total annual depletion.
