Annual Deficit

The 1922 Compact negotiators assumed an average virgin flow of ~17.5–18.0 MAF during an anomalously wet decade (1905–1922). Modern hydrology establishes true sustainable yield at ~12.4 MAF, producing an annual structural overdraft of ~5.1 Million Acre-Feet.
