Question Mark #1 105-mi Pool

Lake Powell Slackwater Reservoir

Why no gages? Traditional streamgages require measurable water velocity. In a 500-foot-deep reservoir pool, water becomes still slackwater (under 0.01 ft/s), making open-channel discharge measurement impossible.

Solution: Inflows are measured upstream at Cisco and Green River; reservoir storage is calculated directly from surface water elevation readings at Glen Canyon Dam.

Question Mark #2 137-mi Canyon

Grand Canyon Granite Gorges

Why no gages? Sheer 1,000-ft vertical cliffs with zero road access, power, or telemetry backhaul. USGS can only service gages where raft logistics permit (Phantom Ranch at Mile 88; Diamond Creek road at Mile 225).

Hydrologic Reality: An 18 to 24-hour wave transit delay occurs between these stations. Flow is governed by Glen Canyon releases; no intermediate gages physically exist.

Question Mark #3 85-mi Pool

Lake Mead Slackwater Reservoir

Why no gages? Identical to Lake Powell: an impounded slackwater pool where water is held in dead storage and active capacity.

Solution: USGS operates station #09404208 at Grand Wash Cliffs (the delta entering the lake); Lake Mead storage is governed by USBR Hoover Dam pool telemetry.
