1. Statutory Compacts & International Treaties (Paper Water Allocations)

The foundational legal compacts dividing interstate entitlements across the seven basin states and the Republic of Mexico

Rate Bridge: 1 MAF/yr ≈ 1,381.16 cfs
1922 Colorado River Compact 42 Stat. 171
7.50 MAF Upper • 7.50 MAF Lower

Article III(a) (Page 2) apportioned 7.50 MAF/yr each to the Upper and Lower Basins as regional totals (no per-state split). Art. III(d) mandates the Upper Division states shall not cause Lees Ferry flow to fall below 75.0 MAF over any consecutive 10-year period.

1948 Upper Basin Compact 63 Stat. 31
CO 51.75% • UT 23% • WY 14% • NM 11.25%

Article III(a) (Pages 2–3) grants Arizona a fixed 50,000 AF/yr (0.05 MAF) and divides the remainder by percentage—not fixed MAF—among CO (51.75%), UT (23.0%), WY (14.0%), and NM (11.25%). At the nominal 7.45 MAF paper cap, those percentages equate to 3.86, 1.71, 1.04, and 0.84 MAF/yr; actual CY 2000–2024 use averaged 3.90 MAF/yr across states (+0.47 CRSP evap).

1928 BCPA & Decree 45 Stat. 1057
CA 4.40 MAF • AZ 2.80 MAF • NV 0.30 MAF

Boulder Canyon Project Act § 4(a) (45 Stat. 1058–1059, Pages 2–3) and the 1964 Supreme Court Decree in Arizona v. California (373 U.S. 546) set fixed Lower Basin mainstream volumes: California 4.40 MAF, Arizona 2.80 MAF, and Nevada 0.30 MAF, excluding Gila River tributaries.

1944 US-Mexico Treaty TS 994
1.50 MAF/yr Treaty Allocation

Treaty Series 994 allocates 1.50 MAF/yr to Mexico at the Northerly and Southerly International Boundaries (Morelos Dam), subject to Minute 323 / 330 shortage reductions. CY 2000–2024 measured boundary deliveries (USBR LC CUL Tab 'Mexico') averaged 1.68 MAF/yr.
